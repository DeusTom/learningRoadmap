Audited strict dependency roadmap · v2.0

From computer foundations to Windows reverse engineering and cryptojacking analysis

A one-resource-at-a-time curriculum built for minimal confusion. Every formal resource is locked until the previous resource is mastered; Windows enters as soon as its genuine systems prerequisites are ready, while unrelated engineering and breadth material is deferred rather than allowed to block specialization.
