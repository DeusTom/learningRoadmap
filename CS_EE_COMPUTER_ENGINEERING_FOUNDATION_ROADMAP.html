Three-part structure

Importance is not chronology.

The three parts classify importance and study intensity. The dependency graph controls when a resource may begin. A supporting resource can therefore be mandatory before a later core resource.

unlocks EE-002 · Signals and Systems (MIT 6.003)

The learner has the mathematics and circuit foundation needed for transform, convolution, sampling, stability, and feedback.
